About

Elegance and energy

More than a DJ, she delivers a full-stage experience; fusing music, live drumming, violin, and movement into a captivating performance that seamlessly blends elegance and energy. Trained in Singapore and the USA, Chelsea Yu is known for crafting genre-bending sets tailored to every crowd and occasion.

A 2-time National DJ Competition Champion, she brings world-class intensity to the decks, no matter the event. Whether creating a refined ambiance or igniting a high-energy dance floor, Chelsea ensures every moment sounds and feels exceptional.
